# Golden retriever

Atlas RGBA generado con la herramienta integrada de imágenes (sin usar la clave
de la aplicación). Personaje realista, no antropomórfico; fondo transparente.
El archivo original se conserva sin recortes destructivos. Qt extrae y escala
los fotogramas al cargar, conserva el alfa y prepara ambas direcciones en caché.

## Distribución: cuatro columnas, tres filas

1. Quieto, hablando, acariciado, dormido.
2. Cuatro fases del paseo.
3. Caída, preparación del salto, salto extendido, ápice del salto.

Se utiliza una escala común por animación y un lienzo de 152 × 112 píxeles
lógicos. Las poses apoyadas comparten línea del suelo. El gato original no se modifica.

## Prompt de la versión seleccionada

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: production 2D desktop pet SPRITE ATLAS, ONE image with 4 columns by 3 rows, twelve equal square cells, landscape 4:3 ratio. Genuine transparent background with alpha, NO checkerboard, no floor, no shadows, no grid lines, no labels or text. Same friendly realistic young adult golden retriever in every cell, natural animal anatomy, warm honey-gold medium fluffy fur, floppy ears, brown eyes, black nose, no collar or accessories, NOT anthropomorphic, not cartoon. Entire animal in every cell including tail and paws, substantial transparent gutters so frames never touch or cross cell boundaries. Consistent side camera facing RIGHT, slight three-quarter view only for seated poses. Same anatomical scale within all cells; all paws baseline at 88% cell height when grounded, tall seated ear top at 16%; horizontal poses naturally shorter, not enlarged. Row1 left to right: (1) idle sitting alert mouth closed; (2) talking sitting in IDENTICAL position with mouth open in cheerful pant; (3) being petted sitting IDENTICAL position eyes closed blissful head slightly tilted no hand; (4) sleeping curled on floor with head on paws. Row2: four distinct sequential WALK CYCLE poses side view facing right, body stays same size and location, feet alternate realistically: left front forward/right rear forward; passing with legs under torso; right front forward/left rear forward; opposite passing. Row3 left to right: (1) descending/falling legs reaching downward; (2) crouch preparing jump; (3) extended leap toward upper right with front paws extended forward; (4) jump apex front paws beginning to tuck. Clean detailed photorealistic cutout fur matching a realistic desktop siamese cat companion. ALL TWELVE cells complete, no duplicates except stable body identity, sharp readable at small size.
CRITICAL REVISION: keep every complete animal strictly INSIDE its own equal grid cell with a generous 12% transparent gutter on ALL FOUR sides. No pixel of any animal may cross a cell border, especially walking tails. Shrink the whole animals as necessary. No colored fringe, no red/yellow glow or scattered debris, no background pixels outside animal. Precise regular grid of 4 columns x 3 rows. Each cell has plenty of transparent breathing room. Walking 4 frames must include passing poses with front legs visibly vertical versus forward-reaching contact poses. Preserve same dog in all frames.
